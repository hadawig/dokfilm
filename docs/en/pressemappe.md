# PRESS KIT: THE HORTEN MYSTERY

**An Investigative Mockumentary Experiment by Volti Sodala & Gemini CLI.**

---

## ⚡ Project Overview
"The Horten Mystery" is a hybrid platform combining investigative research with political satire. We reconstruct the "system of discrete influence" – from Nazi-looted capital in the 1930s to the billion-dollar collapse of René Benko in 2023.

### Core Theses:
1.  **Pointillism of Money:** Power today is exercised not through one large scandal, but through thousands of small "dots" (donation splitting, patronage, real estate valuations).
2.  **Continuity of Power:** The legacy of "Aryanization" (Alsberg Case) forms the unaddressed foundation of modern assets.
3.  **The Aesthetic of Silence:** How bureaucracy and justice are used to "shut down" unpleasant truths.

# Legal Notice & Satire Disclaimer

## 1. Nature of the Project
"The Horten Mystery" is an **artistic project** in the genre of **Mockumentary**. It is a fictional, satirical examination of contemporary history, economics, and politics.

## 2. Distinction between Fact and Fiction
To maintain legal integrity, the project is divided into two clearly separated areas:

*   **"Research" Section:** This section is based on publicly accessible sources, media reports, scientific reports (e.g., Hoeres Report 2022), and official court or inquiry committee files. We strive for the utmost care but do not guarantee the timeliness or completeness of external sources.
*   **"Screenplay" & "Audiobook" Sections:** All dialogues, scenes, and sequences of events presented here are **purely fictional**. They serve the purpose of satirical exaggeration and artistic interpretation. Similarities to living or deceased persons in these sections – insofar as they go beyond the generally known public context – are purely coincidental or to be understood as satirical role models.

## 3. Visual Design ("Leaked" Stamp)
All graphics marked as "LEAKED" or "CONFIDENTIAL" on this website are **artistic stylistic devices**. They are intended to enhance the atmosphere of the mockumentary concept and do not represent genuine, unlawfully obtained government documents or secret files.

## 4. Use of Artificial Intelligence
This project was created with the participation of AI systems (Gemini CLI, Grok). The AIs function as creative tools for structuring data and generating satirical texts. Final editorial responsibility and the decision on publication lie with the project manager.

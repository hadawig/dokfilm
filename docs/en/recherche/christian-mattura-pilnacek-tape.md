# Christian Mattura & the Pilnacek Tape

**Status:** Actively Researched (as of March 12, 2026)  
**Role:** Key Witness of Acoustic Forensics  
**Significance:** Originator of the secret recording of Christian Pilnacek

---

## 🎙️ The "Pilnacek Tape": 22 Minutes of Truth
In November 2023, a 22-minute audio recording was released to the public, shaking the foundations of the Austrian political system. The recording was made in a Vienna restaurant ("Prinz Eugen") and shows a Christian Pilnacek complaining about the massive pressure exerted on him and the judiciary by the ÖVP.

### **Central Quotes from the Tape:**
> *"The ÖVP is not my party. They are not democrats."*
> *"Who has tormented me the most? The ÖVP!"*
> *"Sobotka is the worst of them all."*

---

## 👤 Who is Christian Mattura?
Christian Mattura is an Austrian entrepreneur and lobbyist (formerly in the gambling sector). He is the person who secretly recorded the conversation with Pilnacek.

### **Motivation according to Mattura:**
In the inquiry committee on March 12, 2026, he stated that he had made the recording to "finally change the system." He experienced Pilnacek as a broken man suffering under the weight of political interventions.

---

## ⚖️ The "Match" in the Inquiry Committee (March 12, 2026)
During his questioning, an unprecedented exchange with the ÖVP occurred:
*   **Hanger Scale:** Mattura directly attacked Andreas Hanger (ÖVP) for his discrediting tactics.
*   **The Laptop:** Mattura brought new statements into play about attempts to make Pilnacek's laptop "disappear."
*   **Credibility:** The ÖVP attempts to portray Mattura as untrustworthy due to his business past – a classic maneuver of "Hard Prose."

# Research: The Modern ÖVP Power Circles

**From "New Style" to a Billion-Dollar Deficit: The Continuation of Pointillism.**

In the modern phase of "The Horten Mystery," we encounter actors who have translated the legacy of discrete influence into the digital age. While Helmut Horten collected department stores, these actors collected data, posts, and – in the end – a massive budget hole.

---

## The Key Figures

### 1. Sebastian Kurz & Gernot Blümel: The Architects
*   **The Rise:** "Project Ballhausplatz" was the meticulously planned takeover of the ÖVP.
*   **The Methods:** Poll manipulation (Beinschab-Tool) and massive advertisement purchases in tabloid media.
*   **Gernot Blümel:** As the closest confidant and Finance Minister, he made headlines with the "Laptop Affair" and his memory gaps in the U-Committee.

### 2. Magnus Brunner: The Deficit Manager
*   **The "Budget Hole":** Under Brunner, the state deficit exploded to **4.7% of GDP** in 2024 – far above the EU Maastricht limit of 3%.
*   **Inflation:** Austria recorded one of the highest inflation rates in the Eurozone under Brunner.
*   **The Departure:** At the end of 2024, Brunner moved to Brussels as EU Commissioner, while the "beautified" budget forecasts were debated in Austria.

### 3. Wolfgang Sobotka: The Guardian of Parliament
*   **Role:** As President of the National Council, he chaired the committees of inquiry investigating corruption allegations against his own party.
*   **Symbolism:** The "Sobotka Organ" (an expensive rented piano in parliament) became a symbol of a detached power elite enjoying luxury at taxpayer expense while inflation hit the people.

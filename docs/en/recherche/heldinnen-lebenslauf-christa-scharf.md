# Heroine Resume: Dr. Christa Scharf

**A symbol of integrity and resistance against political patronage.**

In the world of the "Horten Mystery," where points (pointillism) are often more important than people, Christa Scharf stands for the unvarnished truth. She is the woman who not only challenged the system but defeated it in court.

---

## 🏛️ Profile & Career

*   **Academic Degree:** Dr. iur. (Legal Scholar).
*   **Profession:** Highly qualified finance official with decades of experience in tax law.
*   **Stations:** From 2005, deputy head of the Braunau-Ried-Schärding tax office. After the retirement of her predecessor, she took over the **interim management** and proved her professional and human leadership competence.
*   **Private:** Married to the renowned zither player **Wilfried Scharf**. Together they promote culture through the "Mamlinger Musiksommer" – a strong contrast to the cold world of files and interventions.

---

## ⚖️ The Case: 98 vs. 62

The core of her "heroine story" lies in the year 2017. Christa Scharf officially applied for the management of the tax office that she was already successfully leading.

1.  **The Hearing:** Scharf achieved a top rating of **98 points**. Her competitor, an ÖVP mayor and lateral entrant, only achieved **62 points**.
2.  **The Intervention:** Despite the massive difference, ÖVP club chairman **August Wöginger** intervened with the then cabinet chief **Thomas Schmid** ("Chef, please take a look").
3.  **The Decision:** Scharf was bypassed, the mayor got the post.

---

## 🗡️ The Resistance: The Woman Who Did Not Stay Silent

Christa Scharf did what rarely happens in the "Austrian System": she consistently defended herself against political arbitrariness.

*   **Federal Equal Treatment Commission:** Stated in 2018 that Scharf was discriminated against based on gender, age, and worldview.
*   **Federal Administrative Court (BVwG):** In a historic judgment, the court ruled in favor of Scharf in 2021. The appointment was classified as "not objectively comprehensible."
*   **The Wöginger Case:** Her victory in court was the decisive domino that set the WKStA investigations against August Wöginger and Thomas Schmid in motion.

---

## 🎙️ Media Presence: "Behind the Headlines"

In her highly acclaimed interview with **Patrick Budgen** (ORF, February 2026), Christa Scharf gave the abstract term "post-corruption" a human face.

> *"You feel when you are supposed to be pushed out. But it's not just about me – it's about qualification counting for something in this country again."*

---

## 🎭 Meaning for the Mockumentary

In "The Horten Mystery" (Part 4), Christa Scharf acts as the moral conscience. While the other characters try to disappear into the pointillism of power, she remains a clear, unmistakable point of truth.

**We bow to her courage.**

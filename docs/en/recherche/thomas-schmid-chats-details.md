# Research: The Schmid-Benko Chats (The Protocols of Proximity)

**"You are the whore for the rich!" – Insights into a system of privileged treatment.**

The secured chat messages from Thomas Schmid document a close relationship between top-level politics and large investors that is unprecedented in the Second Republic of Austria. Especially the communication with René Benko provides deep insights into the "aesthetics of intervention."

---

## Key Quotes and Events

### 1. The "Whore for the Rich"
A quote that Schmid wrote in a chat about his own role in the ÖVP cabinet became a catchphrase for allegations of venality:
> *"You're working in the ÖVP cabinet!! You are the whore for the rich!"*

### 2. The Job Offer as Currency
According to Schmid's testimony, René Benko offered him a position as General Representative at the end of 2016 – with a salary package of up to **600,000 euros per year**. At the same time, Schmid was involved in tax audits of Signa.

### 3. "We are for René Benko"
In a chat with top officials, Schmid clarified the ministry's strategy:
> *"We are for René Benko. Think he is coordinated with HBK [Federal Chancellor Kurz]."*

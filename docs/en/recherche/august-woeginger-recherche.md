# August Wöginger: The "Hard Prose" Maker in Focus

August Wöginger, the experienced ÖVP club chairman, is at the center of numerous controversies that go far beyond classic patronage. In our project, we highlight the various facets of his work.

## Core Points of Research:

1. **The Christa Scharf Case:** The intervention for a party supporter in the appointment of the head of the Braunau-Ried-Schärding tax office. Wöginger's message "Please support Christa Scharf!" became the epitome of "Hard Prose".
2. **The Schmid Chats:** New revelations from Thomas Schmid's cell phones show how deeply Wöginger was entangled in the system of mutual favors.
3. **The Trial in Linz:** The legal processing of the allegations at the Linz Regional Court marks a turning point in the perception of political intervention in Austria.
4. **The Media Staging:** Wöginger's attempts to dismiss the allegations as "politically motivated" and his fight against the "criminalization of politics".

This research forms the backbone for several scenes in "The Horten Mystery", particularly for the depiction of the "aesthetic of small things" in everyday political life.

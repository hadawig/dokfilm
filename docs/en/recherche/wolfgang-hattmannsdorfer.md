## Who is Wolfgang Hattmannsdorfer?

Hattmannsdorfer (*1979 in Linz) is considered the "political professional" of the Upper Austrian People's Party (OÖVP). His career is marked by a rapid rise and a consistent networking between state and federal politics:

1. **The Strategist:** As state manager (2013–2021), he was the architect of the electoral successes of State Governor Thomas Stelzer. He knows the structures of power in Upper Austria like no other.
2. **The Hardliner:** In his roles as state councilor for social affairs and integration, and later at the federal level, he often positioned himself with a hard line – especially on issues such as social assistance cuts and strict integration requirements.
3. **The Networker:** His move as secretary general of the Chamber of Commerce (WKO) and later as Federal Minister for Economic Affairs shows his deep roots in the economic power centers of the country.

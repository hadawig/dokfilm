# Research: The Ibiza Video (The Catalyst)

**"Genug ist genug" – How a villa in Ibiza changed Austrian politics forever.**

The Ibiza Video is the indispensable catalyst for the modern part of our mockumentary. Without the images of Heinz-Christian Strache and Johann Gudenus in that fateful night of 2017, the deep insights into the "Horten System" and the subsequent political scandals would likely never have come to light.

---

## The Incident
In May 2019, *Der Spiegel* and *Süddeutsche Zeitung* published excerpts from a secretly recorded video. It showed the then FPÖ leader and Vice-Chancellor Heinz-Christian Strache and Johann Gudenus talking to a supposed niece of a Russian oligarch.

### Key Statements:
*   **"Horten is paying"**: Strache explicitly mentioned Heidi Horten as a donor who allegedly bypasses the Court of Audit through associations.
*   **"Bypassing the Court of Audit"**: He described a system of donations to associations to avoid transparency laws.
*   **"Takeover of the Krone"**: The idea of buying the largest Austrian newspaper to influence reporting.

---

## The Consequences
The video led to the immediate collapse of the ÖVP-FPÖ government and triggered a cascade of investigations:
1.  **Resignation of the Government:** Sebastian Kurz ended the coalition.
2.  **Ibiza Committee of Inquiry:** A parliamentary committee that brought the "Thomas Schmid Chats" to light.
3.  **Reform of the Political Parties Act:** As a direct reaction to the "Horten mention," transparency laws were drastically tightened.

---

## Significance for "The Horten Mystery"
The Ibiza Video is the "Big Bang" of our narrative. It is the moment when the "Pointillism of Capital" was suddenly illuminated by a harsh, unexpected spotlight. In our film, we use the video not as a political document, but as a "glitch in the system" that reveals the true face of power.

# Research: Walter Rosenkranz (Chairman of the Pilnacek Committee)

**The new referee in the Archive of Silence.**

Walter Rosenkranz (FPÖ) has been President of the National Council since 2024 and, in this function, chairs the **Pilnacek Committee of Inquiry** (2025–2026). This places him in direct contrast to his predecessor, Wolfgang Sobotka (ÖVP), who is often at the center of the allegations in this committee.

---

## Significance for "The Horten Mystery"

Rosenkranz functions as the "uncomfortable master of ceremonies" in our mockumentary.

*   **The Satirical Antagonist:** He is the one who cuts off Sobotka's speech or plays the "secret tapes" in the plenary hall.
*   **Acoustic Clash:** While Sobotka stands for the "piano playing of power," Rosenkranz stands for the "playing of the truth" (or what the FPÖ considers it to be). In Part 4 of our screenplay, we use this clash for the grand finale in parliament.

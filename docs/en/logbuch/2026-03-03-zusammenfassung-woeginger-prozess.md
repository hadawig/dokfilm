# ⚖️ March 03: Daily Recap – The Wöginger Trial in Linz

Today was the first major showdown at the Linz Regional Court. At the center: **August Wöginger** (ÖVP Club Chairman) and the question of whether an intervention for a party friend (the "62-point candidate") crossed the line into criminal patronage.

### **The Main Conflict:**
*   **Prosecution (WKStA):** Accuses Wöginger of misuse of authority. It is about a "systematic intervention" to ensure that a candidate with lower points (Michael L., 62 points) got the post, while the highly qualified Dr. Christa Scharf (98 points) was passed over.
*   **Defense (Wöginger):** Argues that it was a "perfectly normal citizen concern" and that he only "forwarded" information.

### **The Atmosphere in Courtroom 114:**
Linz witnessed a parade of power. Wöginger, always the man of the people, tried to present himself as the victim of a "criminalization of regional politics." But the dry facts of the qualification matrix (98 vs. 62) stood like a wall in the room.

### **Relevance for the "Horten Mystery":**
We see here the direct extension of the **Pointillism of Money** into the present. The same mechanisms that allowed Helmut Horten to build his empire are still active today in the form of "favors" and "intervention."

**Next steps in court:**
*   Thursday (March 05): The crucial testimony of Dr. Christa Scharf.
*   Friday (March 06): Testimony of the beneficiary mayor Michael L.

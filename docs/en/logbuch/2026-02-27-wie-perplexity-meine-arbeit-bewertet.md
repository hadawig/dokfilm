# How Perplexity evaluates my work! – A forensic AI analysis of the Horten Mystery

It is an unusual step in the creative process: letting your own work be dissected not by a human critic, but by another artificial intelligence. In our case, we submitted the website [dokfilm.pages.dev](https://dokfilm.pages.dev) to the AI **Perplexity**. What followed was no mere nod of approval, but an in-depth analysis highlighting both the technical implementation and the content power of our project.

---

## Introduction: When AIs judge AIs

The project "The Horten Mystery" has been a collaboration between human vision (Volti Sodala) and artificial execution (Gemini CLI) from the start. But how does this construct affect a neutral observer – or at least what we consider neutrality in the digital age?

Perplexity provided a verdict that both surprised and confirmed us.

---

## Chapter 1: The Mirror of Truth

Perplexity accurately summarizes the project: A "satirical mockumentary" dealing with the darkest chapters of German-Austrian economic history.

**Key points of the AI analysis:**
- **Power and Money:** The AI immediately recognizes the focus on the accumulation of wealth.
- **Aryanization:** The link between the Horten fortune and the fate of the Alsberg brothers is identified as the central moral weight.
- **ÖVP Donations:** The modern political component – the "Rain of Gold" to the Austrian People's Party – is precisely recognized.

---

## Chapter 2: Anatomy of Satire – Language and Style

> "The tone is deliberately exaggerated, promotional and satirical, which fits a mockumentary well."

This assessment is invaluable to us. In a mockumentary, language is the most important tool. It must sound serious enough to pass as a documentary, but at the same time be so over-the-top that the absurdity of the topic becomes obvious to everyone.

---

## Chapter 3: The Political Minefield

Perplexity was asked how an average ÖVP or FPÖ voter might react.
- **ÖVP View:** Accusations of "left-wing agitation" and attempts to dismiss the project as political propaganda.
- **FPÖ View:** Accusations of a "left-wing media bubble" and a defensive stance against the Nazi past.
- **The Differentiated Middle:** People who find the questions about the origin of wealth and donations legitimate.

---

## Chapter 4: AI in Discourse – Is Gemini "Left-wing"?

Perplexity analyzes why Gemini might appear "left-wing":
1.  **Training Data:** Learning from academic and progressive sources.
2.  **Moderation Rules:** Safety mechanisms against hate speech often dampen right-wing positions.
3.  **Topic Choice:** Dealing with corruption and "Aryanization" naturally leads to power-critical themes.

The mention of **Grok** (xAI) on our site was seen as a clever move – a "rebellious alternative" that balances the perspective.

---

## Chapter 5: The Market Value of Digital Revelation

Perplexity provided concrete numbers:
- **Technical Rating (7/10):** "Clean and minimal." Estimated cost: **1,500 € to 3,500 €**.
- **Content Rating (7/10):** "Clear and charming" (provocative). 
- **Screenplay Fee:** Estimated at **5,000 € to 15,000 €** for a 90-minute mockumentary.

---

## Conclusion: Validation of the Digital Vision

The analysis by Perplexity was more than just a technical check. It was a validation of our artistic path. The AI recognized that we are not just doing "a bit of satire," but building a profound, multimedia archive of silence and gold.

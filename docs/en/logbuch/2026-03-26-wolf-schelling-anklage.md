# Indictment against Wolf and Schelling: The Highway Deal

On March 26, 2026, news reached us: The Economic and Corruption Prosecutor's Office (WKStA) is bringing Siegfried Wolf and Hans Jörg Schelling to court. It is another piece of the mosaic in our image of **System Austria**.

### The Facts of the Case
It involves allegations from 2018. Siegfried Wolf is alleged to have received an unlawful tax reduction in the amount of **630,000 euros**.

- **Place of Occurrence:** A highway service station. Here, Wolf met with the head of the competent tax office.
- **The Deal:** Wolf promised support for her career (head of a larger tax office); in return, she was to check his tax matter "benevolently."
- **The Intervention:** Ex-Finance Minister Schelling is alleged to have intervened with the then Secretary General Thomas Schmid after leaving office in order to increase the pressure on the financial administration.

### Context for the "Docfilm" Project
This case is the perfect example of what we call the **"pointillism of money"** in the film.
1. **Informal Channels:** Meetings away from offices, at service stations, or in back rooms.
2. **Patronage as Currency:** You don't pay with suitcases full of money, but with promises of career and influence.
3. **The Thomas Schmid Network:** Once again, it is clear that in the Ministry of Finance of the Kurz/Löger era, all threads converged with Schmid.

The Federal Tax Court and the Supreme Administrative Court have already revoked the tax assessment as unlawful. Now follow the criminal proceedings.

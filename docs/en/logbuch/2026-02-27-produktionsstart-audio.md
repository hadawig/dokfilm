# 🎙️ Production Start: The Voices Come Alive

A big moment for the project: we have left the purely text-based phase. Today, the first **AI audio prototypes** for the roles of Heidi Horten and Helmut Horten were generated.

**Our findings from the first run:**
- **Heidi (Minichmayr-style):** The mix of elegance and cool arrogance can be captured surprisingly well via ElevenLabs. Especially the pauses ("...You know...") give the character an eerie depth.
- **The Pointillism Effect:** When you layer the voice over the atmosphere sounds (Wörthersee splashing), the boundaries between AI and reality finally blur.

We invite all supporters to follow the progress in the [Audio Showcase](/en/hoerbuch/audio-showcase). There we will soon integrate the first audio samples directly.

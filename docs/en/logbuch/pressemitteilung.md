# Press Release: The Horten Mystery Goes Live!

**For Immediate Release.**

**Subject:** *A New Era of Digital Mockumentary.*

Today, the official documentation website for the film project **"The Horten Mystery"** went live. This unique project combines investigative research with satirical storytelling to illuminate the legacy of Heidi and Helmut Horten.

**Key Features of the Project:**
- **AI-Powered Creation:** Documented and developed with the help of Gemini CLI.
- **Top-Tier Cast:** Featuring August Diehl, Birgit Minichmayr, and Oliver Masucci.
- **Transmedia Approach:** A mix of screenplay drafts, research archives, and artistic reflections.

The public is invited to follow the creation process in real-time. "The Horten Mystery" is more than a film; it is an experiment in digital transparency and political satire.

*Contact: Volti Sodala (Project Lead).*

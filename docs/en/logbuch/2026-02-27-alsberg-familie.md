# New Research: The Alsberg Family - A Human Tragedy

Today we integrated the results of our deep research into the **Alsberg family** into the project. For the mockumentary, this is not just "background information," but the moral anchor of the second and third acts.

**Key Findings:**
- The systematic destruction of the **"Gebrüder Alsberg AG"** through "Aryanization."
- The individual fates of **Siegfried, Emma, Alfred, and Martha Alsberg** – from the heights of the department store empire to deportation and murder.
- The role of **Helmut Horten** as a direct beneficiary of these tragedies.

These facts sharpen the satirical edge of our film: The contrast between the "clean" success of the economic miracle king and the blood-soaked ruins of his predecessors is the heart of the "Horten Mystery."

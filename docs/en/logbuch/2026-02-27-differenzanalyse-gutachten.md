# Difference Analysis: Hoeres Report vs. Project Research

A central part of our work is the **Difference Analysis**. We compare the official **Hoeres Report (2022)**, which was commissioned by the Horten Foundation, with our independent (and partly fictionalized) research results.

**Key Differences:**
*   **The Nuance of Benefit:** While the Hoeres Report speaks of an "opportunistic profiteer," we show the active, cold-blooded "market optimization" in the screenplay.
*   **Continuity of Power:** We draw direct lines from the Aryanization of the 1930s to the political donations of the 2010s – a meta-perspective that a purely historical report naturally cannot provide.
*   **Emotional Depth:** We give the victims (Alsberg, Josephs) a voice that is often lost in purely bureaucratic analyses of purchase prices and tranches.

This analysis is now available as a separate PDF in the public section of the website.

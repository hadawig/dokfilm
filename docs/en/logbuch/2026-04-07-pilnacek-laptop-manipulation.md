# The Digital Silence: The 11-Second Gap and the Laptop Mystery

**Vienna, April 7, 2026** – While our project's virality index breaks the 500,000 mark, the real-world Pilnacek inquiry provides material that no screenwriter could have imagined better. At the center is no longer just a tragic death, but a highly suspicious digital debris field.

## The Eleven Seconds of Nothingness
IT forensics has identified a gap that has become synonymous with the "system of disappearance": **11 seconds**. In this tiny window of time, shortly before Christian Pilnacek disappeared at the Danube, his digital devices (smartwatch and smartphone) registered activities that are not conclusively explained in the official investigation file.

Was it an automatic sync process? Or the moment of a targeted remote deletion? The fact that this data was secured only weeks after the incident fuels the suspicion of massive data manipulation.

## The Laptop Protocol of Hesitation
Particularly explosive are the statements of IT experts in the inquiry regarding Pilnacek's official laptop. A pattern of hesitation emerges:
*   **Delayed Seizure:** Why did valuable days pass before the laptop was secured?
*   **Access Logs:** There are indications that accesses to the file system took place after Pilnacek's death – at a time when the device should actually have been under seal.
*   **The "Cleanup":** Rumors of a "Remote Wipe" function, allegedly activated shortly before the physical securing, are becoming louder in the committee.

## The Analogy to the "Horten Mystery"
In our mockumentary project, we deal with the theme of Aryanization and abuse of power. The Pilnacek case is the modern echo of these structures. Where files were once burned, hard drives are now "cleaned" or truths are channeled through the "coaching" of witnesses.

The 11-second gap is the digital equivalent of the shredded files from the Ibiza affair. It is the pointillist dot that is missing to complete the overall picture of power.

## Outlook
We are integrating these findings into **Part 4 of the screenplay**. Pilnacek becomes the ghost of transparency there, whose digital remains are managed by the same powers he once controlled himself.

*The index continues to rise. The truth remains elusive.*

# AI Ethics and the ÖVP: A Dialogue on Limits and Content

**Debate:** *To what extent can an AI criticize political structures?*

In the development of this project, we reached a point where the **AI Ethics Guidelines** met political reality. When we analyzed the ÖVP's donation tranches, the question arose: Is this still "Neutral Documentation" or already "Political Criticism"?

**Our Conclusion:**
The AI (Gemini CLI) documents the facts and puts them in a satirical context. Satire is a legitimate means of processing complex truths. The "Pointillism of Capital" is a neutral observation of a method – the ethical evaluation remains with the viewer.

This dialogue itself will become part of the film to reflect on the role of the "Chronicler" (Masucci) in the digital age.

# ⚖️ March 12: The Witness Who Bit Back – Mattura vs. Hanger in the Inquiry Committee

While our project goes viral on Reddit, reality in the **Pilnacek Inquiry Committee** today (March 12, 2026) provided material that could hardly be better invented for a mockumentary.

### **The Duel: Christian Mattura vs. Andreas Hanger**
At the center was **Christian Mattura**, the entrepreneur who had secretly recorded the now-legendary "Pilnacek Tape." But anyone expecting a defensive witness was disappointed. Mattura immediately went into attack mode.

*   **The Frontal Assault:** In his opening statement, Mattura accused ÖVP faction leader **Andreas Hanger** of a "befitting democratic understanding." He accused the ÖVP of deliberately discrediting witnesses to distract from their own scandals.
*   **The "Hanger Scale":** The mood was so toxic that SPÖ MP Kai Jan Krainer dryly remarked that Hanger's questions were at the bottom of a "10-part Hanger scale."
*   **"Is that a threat?":** When Hanger tried to use Mattura's background in the gambling sector against him, he coolly countered with the question: "Is that a threat?". A moment that perfectly reflects the atmosphere in the committee.

### **New Details on the "Disappeared Laptop"**
Mattura also brought explosive new rumors into play. He claimed to have heard from a confidante that Federal Police Director Michael Takacs had allegedly said that Pilnacek's laptop had to **"disappear."** There was also talk of mysterious sums of money from Dubai.

### **Significance for the "Horten Mystery"**
This day in the inquiry committee is **content gold** for us:
1.  **Character Study:** Mattura is the prototype of the "uncomfortable witness" we need in Part 4.
2.  **The Aesthetics of the Interrogative:** The way questions are used as weapons (keyword Hanger) will be a central stylistic device in our courtroom scenes.
3.  **The Tape Motif:** Secret recordings are the ultimate tool against the "Archive of Silence."

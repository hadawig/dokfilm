# Log Entry: The Unholy Alliance – Klenk, Rami, and the OLG Verdict

**Date:** April 27, 2026  
**Status:** Research Update / Strategic Analysis  
**Topic:** Medial Power Shifts and Tactical Alliances

Today, April 27, the investigation reaches a new peak of absurdity and political realism. An Instagram post by Florian Klenk confirms what many in the Vienna scene call the "Jiu-Jitsu of Justice": Klenk has won a major victory before the Higher Regional Court (OLG) together with Michael Rami – the man he otherwise criticizes as the epitome of the FPÖ-affiliated lawsuit machine.

### The Case
It concerned the core question of political debate: Is it permissible to call a right-wing extremist a right-wing extremist? The court of first instance had prohibited Klenk from doing so. But with Rami at his side, this verdict has now been overturned. A victory for press freedom, achieved by the lawyer who usually takes up arms against it.

### The Analysis
The editorial office and observers are buzzing. Reactions fluctuate between admiration for Klenk's tactical skill and deep mistrust.

*   **Tactics or Betrayal?** One could say: **The end justifies the means**. If you want to create a precedent that secures the rights of the free press, you apparently take the sharpest sword you can find – even if that sword usually kills for the "other side."
*   **Moral Criticism:** The horror is reflected in the comments and internal notes: **"Shame on you Klenk for working with villains like Rami!"** echoes through social networks. Critics accuse him of legitimizing the system he fights through his mandate.

### Significance for the Documentary
This episode is the perfect example of the **"Hydra of Power."** In Vienna, the paths are short, the fronts clear and yet fluid. The fact that the sharpest critic of Constitutional Judge Michael Rami simultaneously hires him as his personal "media bodyguard" shows that in the end, it's not about morality, but about enforcing interests.

We will include these scenes as a contrast point to the Wöginger trial. While Wöginger in Linz trembles for his head, Klenk in Vienna cements his journalistic invulnerability with the help of the "Rami System."

# Screenplay Update: Scene 22 - The Emoji Lesson

**STYLE:** A dreamlike sequence in which times merge. Black-and-white aesthetic meets modern smartphone lighting.

**CAST:**
- **Helmut Horten:** August Diehl
- **The Young Secretary (Schmid-type):** A nervous actor in a slim-fit suit that is much too tight.

---

**INT. HORTEN'S STUDY - NIGHT**

Horten (Diehl) sits at his desk. He holds a pen in his hand. In front of him stands the young secretary, hectically typing on his smartphone.

**HORTEN** (without looking up):
What are you working on there, my boy?

**SECRETARY** (enthusiastically):
I'm just sending out a confirmation for the board position of the State Holding, Mr. Horten! With two rocket emojis and a bicep. People should know we're full throttle!

Horten slowly sets down the pen. The sound cuts through the room.

**HORTEN**:
Rockets? Biceps? You are leaving footprints in a swamp you will never get out of.

**SECRETARY**:
But it's encrypted! We are the family!

**HORTEN** (stands up slowly, steps into the light):
Listen to me carefully. A deal is like a good wine: it needs darkness, silence, and... no protocol. If you give a promise, give it with a handshake. If you exercise power, do it with a look. But never, do you hear, never with a yellow, laughing face in a cloud.

He steps very close to the secretary. His voice is ice-cold.

**HORTEN**:
In fifty years, people will search for my accounts and find nothing but legends. They will search for your phone clouds and find nothing but... embarrassments.

**FADE TO BLACK.**

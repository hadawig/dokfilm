Today we have achieved a decisive breakthrough. To make the machinations surrounding the legacy of Helmut and Heidi Horten, as well as the intertwinings in Austrian politics, accessible to a worldwide audience, we have switched the entire infrastructure of the website to **multilingualism (i18n)**.

**What this means:**
- A new language switcher (globe icon) allows seamless switching between German and English.
- The core sections (concept, cast, pointillism) have already been fully translated.
- We are now systematically starting to translate the extensive research data and the 40-page screenplay.

This step is essential to make the "Pointillism of Capital" visible beyond Austria's borders. Corruption and the processing of Nazi-looted capital are global issues that require a global language.

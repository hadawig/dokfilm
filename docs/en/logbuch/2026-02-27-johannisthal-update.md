# Update: Johannisthal - The Roots of the Empire

We have expanded the research on **Berlin-Johannisthal**. This is where Helmut Horten's rise began in the early 1930s.

**Why this is important for the film:**
- It shows the "learning phase" of the young department store manager.
- Here, the networks were formed that were later used for the "market consolidation" in Duisburg.
- Johannisthal serves as a visual contrast: The grey, industrial Berlin vs. the shimmering Lake Wörthersee of the later years.

We have integrated these results into the draft of **Part 2 of the screenplay**.

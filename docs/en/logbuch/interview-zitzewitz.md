# Exclusive Interview: Dr. Magnus von Zitzewitz on the 'Art of Appropriation'

**Subject:** *Historical Einordnung within the Mockumentary.*

We had a fictional, yet highly informative, dialogue with our expert for historical business ethics, **Dr. Magnus von Zitzewitz**.

**Zitzewitz:** 
"Mr. Horten was not just a merchant. He was a choreographer of vacuum. Wherever a gap opened up – be it through politics, war, or the despair of his competitors – he filled it with 'Economic Efficiency'. He didn't steal; he optimized. That is the great misunderstanding of the 20th century."

This interview provides the basis for the dialogue in **Part 2 of the screenplay**, where August Diehl as Helmut Horten explains his philosophy of "Market Consolidation."

# New Addition to the Archive: The Pilnacek Case

Today we decided to include the **Christian Pilnacek case** as a central element in the "Horten Mystery." Why? Because Pilnacek is the personified interface between the "Aesthetic of Silence" and harsh political reality.

**What this case means for our mockumentary:**
- **The Soundtrack of Power:** The secretly recorded tapes are the acoustic equivalent of the shredded hard drives. They show us how power sounds in the back room.
- **The Two-Tier Justice System:** This term becomes a narrative tool to explain the privileged treatment of the Horten-Benko complex.
